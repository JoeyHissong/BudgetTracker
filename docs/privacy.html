Privacy Policy

Budget Planner

← Back to home

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Budget Planner ("the app") is a local-only budgeting app for tracking recurring monthly bills. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

🔒 Budget Planner does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Budget Planner has no account system, no backend server, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. The app does not know who you are and cannot identify you.

Data stored on your device

Bills, due dates, categories, payment status, your pay schedule, and your app preferences (currency, theme, etc.) are saved locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us or any third party, and is only accessible to the app itself.

Exporting and importing your data

The app lets you export a copy of your data (bills, pay schedule, and preferences) as a JSON file, and later import that file to restore it. This file is only written to or read from a location you explicitly choose using your device's native file picker — the app does not upload it anywhere or send it to us or any third party.

Third-party services

Budget Planner does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app works entirely offline.

Children's privacy

Budget Planner does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, and is suitable for general audiences.

Deleting your data

Since all data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by removing your bills within the app or by uninstalling the app, which removes all associated local data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub Issues.
